# Data access

The `source_data/` directory contains aggregate study outputs. Frozen model definitions are in `models/`. These are the data actually distributed in this repository.

The 27 public study-generated aggregate tables and five model-definition files identified in [DATA_LICENSE_MANIFEST.tsv](DATA_LICENSE_MANIFEST.tsv) can be downloaded without an application and reused under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DATA.md). The grant is limited to those original outputs and does not change access conditions or licenses for the underlying third-party datasets.

## Not deposited in this release

The two in-house single-cell datasets, individual-level clinical/expression files, raw sequencing files, reconstructed cell matrices, and patient-specific metadata are not publicly deposited here. Their final release scope, consent/institutional conditions, data-reuse license and repository accession remain to be finalized. No established controlled-access process or guaranteed availability on request is claimed.

Third-party checkpoints, force fields and other materials whose redistribution terms have not been resolved are also omitted. Questions about study data: corresponding author Yongchang Wei, weiyongchang8@163.com.
